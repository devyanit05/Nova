# 🌌 Nova

> **A Life Operating System**

---

# Vision

Nova is a **Life Operating System** designed to help people intentionally grow in every aspect of life through one unified platform.

Instead of managing tasks, habits, finances, learning, health, and goals across dozens of disconnected applications, Nova connects them into one intelligent system that provides clarity, structure, and meaningful progress.

Nova isn't built to make people busier.

It is built to help people become better.

---

# Mission

Help people make meaningful progress every single day by transforming scattered habits, goals, knowledge, health, and productivity into one connected experience.

---

# The Problem

Today's life is fragmented.

People use one app for:

- Tasks
- Notes
- Calendar
- Finance
- Habit Tracking
- Learning
- Health
- Journaling
- Goals

Every app solves one small problem.

None of them answer the bigger question:

> **"How is my life going?"**

Nova exists to answer that question.

# Workspaces

The heart of Nova is its Workspaces.

Instead of giving users an empty application, Nova provides thoughtfully designed workspaces that organize every major area of life.

Each workspace comes with an intelligent default template that works immediately while remaining fully customizable.

Users can modify, remove, duplicate, or create their own workspaces over time.

The goal is simple:

> **Provide an exceptional starting point that eventually becomes uniquely theirs.**
