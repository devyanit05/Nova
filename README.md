## ✨ Why Nova?

Modern life is scattered.

- 📅 Tasks live in one app.
- ⏰ Reminders live somewhere else.
- 💰 Expenses are tracked separately.
- 💧 Water intake is forgotten.
- 📖 Learning progress is never measured.
- 🎯 Goals get abandoned halfway.
- 💡 Great ideas disappear into random notes.

Nova brings everything together.

Instead of managing apps...

**You manage your life.**

---

# 🚀 Vision

Nova isn't just another productivity app.

It's a **Life Operating System**—a platform designed to help people intentionally build better habits, stronger skills, healthier routines, and meaningful long-term progress.

Every feature exists for one reason:

> **Help you become the person you're trying to be.**
